BaySickDAW is one fixed fullscreen frame with windows inside it. Nothing you do here makes sound; it is the furniture. Learn it once and every other chapter is easier.

Knowing how hard the app is working

TABBAR-14Performance readoutTransport bar, far right

Three rows of small numbers. Hover them for the legend — that tooltip is the only place these are spelled out in the app.

ReadsMeansWatch for
SYSHow busy the whole computer isGreen under 50%, yellow over 80%
DSPHow much of the audio engine's time budget is being usedOver 80% and you are close to crackles
MEMMemory used, in MBClimbs as you load samples. Normal.
LATDelay added by plugins, in samplesMatters when recording live
UNDAudio dropouts while playingShould stay 0. Anything else means the disk could not keep up

DSP is a percentage of your buffer size, not of your CPU. Raising the buffer in Audio & MIDI Settings (AUD-4) gives the engine a bigger budget, so the same project reads lower. That is the first thing to try when it climbs.

2 · Making a track — the tab families

A tab is one part of your song: a bass, a drum kit, a vocal. Adding a tab is how anything gets made. The ribbon is where tabs live.

A tab and its window are two different things. The tab is the part — it holds the sound, the notes and the mixer strip, and it plays whether or not you are looking at it. The window is just how you look at it. Closing a window never silences anything.

3 · The instruments

The things that actually make sound. Seven of them ship with the app, and you can host other people's as well.

Pick by what you want, not by what sounds clever. BaySickPlayer plays recordings of real instruments — the easiest place to start. BaySickSynth and BaySickBass build sounds from scratch and are the ones to learn on. BaySickSolstice is the deep one; it will still be there when you want it. BaySickGuitars, BaySickBasses and BaySickRustyDrums are sampled real instruments that need no setup.

The deep one. Everything is on one page, grouped into labelled boxes.

Fig. HARM · IMP-57

Stacks copies of every note slightly apart for a thicker sound. VOICES is how many, then PAN, BSPIT and PHASE spread them.
