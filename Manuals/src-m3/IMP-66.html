Applies to OFF-BEAT 16ths only: `(floor(localStart / 0.25) & 1) != 0` — odd 16th index in the PATTERN-LOCAL grid (local, not absolute, so block placement never changes a pattern's feel). The whole note shifts (note-off rides the pushed start).
