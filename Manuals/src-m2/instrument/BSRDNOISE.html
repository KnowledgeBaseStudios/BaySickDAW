The Noises and Clicks section - the kit's incidental sounds: pedal squeaks, stand rattles, percussion extras. Quiet on their own, they are a surprising amount of why a programmed kit reads as a person playing in a room.

MECH NOISES - the hardware's own sounds (pedal action, stand rattle, the machinery of the kit): Close and Overhead levels with a Tune.

PERCUSSION - the percussion extras: Close / Overhead levels, how much they excite the snare's wires (Snare btm), and a Tune.

PAN - one pan per noise source (Snare, Hi-hat, the four toms), so each source's incidental sound sits WITH its drum in the stereo picture even though it is generated here.
