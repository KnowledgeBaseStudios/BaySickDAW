2. Parameters

Invert Feedback: Boolean. Inverts the phase of the signal fed back into the delay line.

Invert Wet: Boolean. Inverts the phase of the processed wet signal before it reaches the output mix.

Dry / Wet / Cross Levels: Floats from -100.0 dB (or -inf) to 0.0 dB. Controls the final output mix.
