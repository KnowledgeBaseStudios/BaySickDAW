## 1. Where to Source Redistributable Samples
The following libraries provide high-quality, legally safe audio that you can redistribute within your software.

* [cite_start]**VSCO 2 Community Edition (Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2):** Includes strings, brass, woodwinds, and **percussion**[cite: 2].
    * [cite_start]**The License:** CC0 (Public Domain), allowing you to repackage and distribute them legally without cost[cite: 3, 4].
* [cite_start]**Virtual Playing Orchestra:** A cohesive blend of several open-source libraries, including VSCO 2[cite: 5].
    * [cite_start]**The License:** Mostly CC0; some specific instruments require attribution (CC-BY)[cite: 6].
* [cite_start]**Salamander Grand Piano:** A deeply sampled Yamaha C5 grand piano[cite: 7].
    * [cite_start]**The License:** CC-BY (Attribution required in your DAW's "About" section)[cite: 8].
* [cite_start]**University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS):** Raw, unedited recordings of nearly every orchestral instrument[cite: 9].
    * [cite_start]**The License:** Public Domain[cite: 10].

---

## 2. Preparation for the Installer
[cite_start]Claude and automated installer scripts require **Direct Download Links** to raw files (usually .zip) rather than navigating landing pages[cite: 11, 12].

1.  [cite_start]**Download and Sort:** Organize your chosen CC0/Public Domain samples into five folders: **Strings, Horns, Woodwinds, Keys, and Percussion**[cite: 13, 14].
2.  [cite_start]**Compress:** Create five individual .zip files[cite: 14].
3.  [cite_start]**Host:** Upload these to a server or storage bucket (e.g., AWS S3, Google Cloud Storage) so they each have a public URL[cite: 15].

### Why This Works
This architecture keeps your initial installer size small while ensuring the user receives the full library upon installation. [cite_start]By specifying the **Percussion** link separately, Claude will ensure the script handles the extra download thread and extraction path accurately[cite: 19].
