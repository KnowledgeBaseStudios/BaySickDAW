3. Baseline Mathematical Logic for Spectral Modules
   * Filters: Standard filters manipulate audio over time. This additive filter manipulates the amplitude array before summing. Let $A_n$ be the amplitude of the $n$-th partial, and $H(f_n)$ be the filter mask function (e.g., a lowpass curve) evaluated at the partial's frequency. The new amplitude $A'_n$ is:
$$A'_n = A_n \times H(f_n)$$
Modulating the filter cutoff simply shifts the index of the mask $H$ relative to the array $A$.
