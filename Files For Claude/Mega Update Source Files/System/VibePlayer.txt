This is a comprehensive architectural blueprint for the VibePlayer, merging your DSP requirements with a multi-articulation, disk-streaming sampler engine capable of parsing both recursive folder structures and `.SFZ` files.

[cite_start]Building a sample-based instrument for a DAW [cite: 27] [cite_start]that integrates advanced DSP with a "Folder of Folders" logic [cite: 29] and `.SFZ` parsing requires extending JUCE's native `Synthesiser` class. [cite_start]Native JUCE does not stream from disk or parse `.SFZ` files out of the box, so we must build a custom `VibeSound` and `SampleManager` to handle Disk Streaming without over-allocating RAM [cite: 38] [cite_start]and Round Robin cycling[cite: 39].

Here is the core C++ architecture.
