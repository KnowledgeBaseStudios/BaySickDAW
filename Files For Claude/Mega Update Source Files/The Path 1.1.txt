*Saturation

 * Bass Relief: Float from 0 to 100. Controls a 350 Hz shelf filter to bypass the tube engine for the low end.

 * Out: Float from -18.0 to +18.0 dB. Final output gain.

*Chorus
 * Delay: Float from 0.0 to 30.0 ms. Sets the minimum delay between the chorus voices.
 * Depth: Float. Controls the chorus delay modulation. The delay will sweep between Delay and Delay + Depth. If zero, the modulation is constant.
 * Stereo: Float from 0 to 360 degrees. Controls the widening of the stereo image. At 0, left and right LFOs are in perfect sync. At 180, left and right are 100% out of phase.
 * LFO 1 Freq / LFO 2 Freq / LFO 3 Freq: Floats. Sets the modulation speed for three independent LFOs.
 * LFO 1 Wave / LFO 2 Wave / LFO 3 Wave: Choices. Sets the waveform of the LFOs (e.g., Sine, Triangle, Multi-sine). The "Multi-sine" option must be mathematically defined as a mix of a normal sine wave and one running at three times its speed.
 * Cross Type: Choice (Process HP / Process LP). Defines whether the plugin works on the lower or higher frequency part of the signal. "Process HP" processes only high frequencies, "Process LP" processes only low frequencies.
 * Cross Cutoff: Float. The cutoff frequency of the crossover filter.

*Compressor
 *Threshold: Float from 0.0 to -60.0 dB. Sets the level at which compression kicks in.
 *Ratio: Float from 0.4 to 30.0 (representing 0.4:1 to 30:1). Controls the amount of gain reduction. Note that values below 1:1 should act as upward expansion.
 *Gain: Float from -30.0 to +30.0 dB. Make-up gain applied after compression.
 *Attack: Float from 0.0 to 400.0 ms. Time to reach full compression.
 *Release: Float from 1.0 to 4000.0 ms. Time to stop acting after the level falls below threshold.
 *Type (Knee): Choice (Hard, Medium, Vintage, Soft, Hard/R, Medium/R, Vintage/R, Soft/R).
 *Hard means compression takes place immediately at the threshold.
 *Soft/Medium/Vintage dictate different curve widths and program-dependent behaviors around the threshold. Ensure the math translates these into an actual knee width (in dB) for the gain computer.

*Delay
 *Input: Wet In (Input level scaling).

 *Delay Model: Choice (Mono, Stereo, Ping-Pong, Off), Stereo Spread (0.0 to 1.0).

1. Band Core Parameters (Per-Band)
Filter Types: OFF, Low Pass, Band Pass, High Pass, Notch, Low Shelf, Peaking (default), High Shelf.

Key: Ability to snap or center the frequency band to the fundamental frequency of a specific musical note.
Mute/Solo: Per-band toggle.

2. Global Audio & DSP Modes
Main Level: Global output gain.
Phase Modes:
Standard (IIR): Minimum phase mode. Fast parameter changes but alters phase.
LIN (Linear Phase): Uses FFT filters. Introduces latency but prevents phase rotation. Includes a "Precision" setting (Lowest to Highest) where higher precision allows steeper slopes at the cost of more latency.
HQ (High Quality) Modes:
Standard: Disabled (Normal standard IIR).
HQ+ (Plus): Decramped consistent bandwidth IIR filters (mimicking ideal analog without digital shallow bandwidth artifacts, no oversampling).
HQL (Legacy): Polyphase downsampling filter (no latency, but phase rotation above 15 kHz and potential inter-sample peaks).
HQE (Enhanced): FIR downsampling filter (1ms latency, accurate peak levels, no additional phase rotation).
Parameter Smoothing:
Minimum Phase Mod Speed: Controls max modulation slew-rate for IIR filters to prevent crackling on fast movements.
Linear Phase Mod Speed: Choose between Normal, Fast, Faster, or Ultra (higher uses more CPU).

3. Monitoring & Analyzers
Monitor Input Source: MID (L+R/2), SIDE (L-R), Left Channel, Right Channel.
Monitor Stage: Input (Pre-EQ), Output (Post-EQ), or Disabled.
Frequency Histogram:
Displays peaks from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.
Range: -60 dB, -90 dB, or -120 dB.
Pivot Slope: 1.5, 3, 4.5 (default), or 6 dB/octave (rotates spectrum around 1 kHz for visual emphasis/de-emphasis).
Frequency Precision: Controls the number of bands used (higher = more visual latency).
Average Mode: OFF (reacts to instantaneous peaks), ON/RMS (shows average peaks over a time window).
Time Smoothing: Controls fall speed (if Average is OFF) or time window length (if Average is ON).
Heatmap:
Top-down intensity view of the frequencies.
High Precision: Toggles precise vertical lines vs. an alternative algorithm that blurs lows but makes center frequencies easier to spot.
Enhanced Frequency: Thinner lines.
Position: Top, Bottom, or Full (over the histogram).
Phase Rotation Display: View the phase rotation caused by IIR filters (+/- 180 degrees scale, aligned with +/- 18 dB labels).

4. Interactive UI & Workflow Features
Band Tokens (Nodes):
Left-click drag: Adjust Frequency (X-axis) and Gain (Y-axis).
Mouse-wheel (or Shift+Drag horizontal): Adjust Bandwidth.
Alt+Click: Reset band.
Ctrl+Click: Fine adjustment.
Right-click menu: Filter Type, Order/Slope, and Key.
Toggle Visibility: Turn tokens ON/OFF.
Band Soloing Options:
"Band solo on shift": Holding Shift and clicking a token momentarily solos the band. Includes an "Inherit bandwidth" option (changes made while soloed apply to the active band upon release).
Solo Modes:
Bandpass: Uses a Steep 8 Bandpass filter to solo the token.
Adaptive: Uses dynamic filters based on the band being soloed (e.g., LP uses HP to solo what's being removed, Peaking uses Bandpass to solo what's being adjusted).
