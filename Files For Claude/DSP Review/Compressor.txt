To make your **CompressorDSP** stand out as a professional-grade tool for **Vibesynth**, you have to move beyond the basic "if signal > threshold" logic. Stock FL effects and high-end plugins use **Logarithmic Domain** processing and **Recursive Ballistics**.

Here is the full breakdown for a professional, analog-modeled Compressor.

---

### 1. The Detection Path (Logarithmic Domain)
Most "cheap" compressors calculate gain in the linear domain (0.0 to 1.0), which sounds aggressive and unmusical. Professional compressors convert the signal to Decibels first.
* **The Logic:** Convert the absolute peak/RMS of the sidechain (or input) to dB.
* **Formula:** $x_{dB} = 20 \cdot \log_{10}(|x| + \epsilon)$ (where $\epsilon$ is a tiny value like $1e-9$ to prevent math errors at silence).

### 2. The Gain Computer (Soft-Knee & Expansion)
This is the "brain" that decides how much to turn the volume down. To get the "Vintage" knee you want, we use a quadratic interpolation.

**The Math (Gain Reduction in dB):**
If your Threshold is $T$, Ratio is $R$, and Knee width is $W$:
* **If $2(x_{dB} - T) < -W$:** Output is $x_{dB}$ (No compression).
* **If $2|x_{dB} - T| \le W$:** Output is $x_{dB} + \frac{(\frac{1}{R} - 1)(x_{dB} - T + \frac{W}{2})^2}{2W}$ (Inside the Knee).
* **If $2(x_{dB} - T) > W$:** Output is $T + \frac{x_{dB} - T}{R}$ (Full compression).

*Note: If $R < 1.0$, this same formula naturally becomes an **Upward Expander**, which is a "pro" feature for adding life back into dull drums.*

### 3. Ballistics (Recursive Exponential Smoothing)
This is where the "sound" of the compressor lives. Clicks and pops happen because the attack/release is too fast or linear. We use **Time Constants** that mimic a capacitor charging.

**The Formula (Coefficient Calculation):**
$$\alpha = 1.0 - e^{-1.0 / (\text{time\_ms} \cdot 0.001 \cdot \text{sampleRate})}$$

**The Recursive Loop:**
```cpp
// Logic for the envelope follower
if (targetGain < lastGain) // Attacking
    currentGain = targetGain + attackCoeff * (lastGain - targetGain);
else // Releasing
    currentGain = targetGain + releaseCoeff * (lastGain - targetGain);
```

### 4. Architecture & Sidechaining
To ensure it’s "FL-ready," the compressor must support an external sidechain buffer.
* **Look-ahead:** Implement a small `juce::dsp::DelayLine` (approx. 2ms to 5ms) on the **main signal**, but NOT on the detector signal. This allows the compressor to "react" to a peak before the peak actually hits the speakers.
* **Stereo Linking:** Calculate the envelope based on the maximum of the Left and Right channels to prevent the stereo image from shifting/wobbling.
