This interface is incredibly dense, but breaking it down into a strict hierarchy will make handing it off to Claude for your C++ generation much smoother. Because you are building a standalone application in JUCE and applying your own custom skeuomorphic textures (like your chicken-head knobs), I have categorized these elements using standard UI component logic. 

You can feed this exact breakdown to Claude to generate the flexbox/grid layouts and the corresponding `juce::Slider`, `juce::ToggleButton`, and `juce::ComboBox` declarations for the project.

Here is the complete topographical map of the interface, divided into its main logical panels:

### **1. Top Left Panel: Timbre, Modulation & Pitch**
This section handles the core signal generation and localized modulation.

* **Phase Group**
    * 2 Standard Knobs: `start`, `rand`
* **Timbre Group**
    * 2 Waveform displays/buttons
    * 1 Large Knob: `MIX`
    * 1 Horizontal Slider: `Fade`
* **Mixer/Routing Matrix**
    * 3 Vertical Sliders: `sub`, `prot`, `clip`
    * 3 Vertical Sliders: `fx`, `vol`, `env`
    * 1 Column of 6 tiny Toggle Buttons (LED style): `auto`, `vel`, and 4 routing shapes
* **Blur Group**
    * 3 Standard Knobs: `mix`, `time`, `harm`
    * 2 Tiny Toggle Buttons (Curve routing)
* **Tremolo (TREM) Group**
    * Waveform selector toggle
    * 3 Standard Knobs: `depth`, `speed`, `gap`
* **Prism Group**
    * 1 Standard Knob: `AMT`
    * 1 Dropdown/Value Box: `mode`
    * 3 Tiny Toggle Buttons: `+`, `-`, `from vol`
* **Harmonizer Group**
    * 2 Standard Knobs: `AMT`, `WIDTH`
    * 1 Tiny Knob: `str`
    * 3 Value/State Boxes: `ofs step`, `shift` (with `1`, `x2` buttons), `gap` (with `1`, `x2` buttons)
* **Pitch Group**
    * 2 Number Input Boxes: `freq`, `detune` (with adjacent fraction selectors `/4`, `/1`)
    * 2 Tiny Toggle Buttons: `oct`, `Hz`
* **Vibrato Group**
    * 1 Waveform selector toggle
    * 3 Standard Knobs: `env`, `depth`, `speed`
* **Legato Group**
    * 2 Standard Knobs: `time`, `limit`
    * 2 Curve Toggle Buttons

### **2. Top Middle Panel: Unison**
A narrow vertical strip dedicated to voice multiplication.

* 1 Value Box: `order` (currently set to 4)
* 1 Dropdown Menu: `Blurred`
* 1 Radio Button: `alt`
* 3 Vertical Sliders: `pan`, `pitch`, `phase`

### **3. Top Right Panel: Filtering Section & Lower FX**
This houses the dual filter architecture and the final effect stages.

* **Filter 1 (Top Row)**
    * 1 Dropdown Menu: `Classic low pass`
    * 1 Envelope Knob: `ENV`
    * 2 Large Main Knobs: `FREQ`, `RES`
    * 2 Standard Knobs: `width` (x2)
    * 3 Tiny Knobs: `kb track`, `ofs`, `osc`
    * 1 Secondary Dropdown: Filter shape (`Classic`)
    * 6 Tiny Toggle Buttons: `adapt`, `oct`, `Hz` (scattered around the main knobs)
* **Filter 2 (Middle Row)**
    * Exact duplicate layout of Filter 1, defaulted to `Classic high pass`.
* **Bottom FX Row**
    * **Pluck:** 1 Standard Knob (`TIME`), 2 Toggle Buttons (`blur`, curve shape)
    * **Phaser:** 1 Dropdown (`Triangle`), 1 Large Knob (`MIX`), 3 Standard Knobs (`WIDTH`, `OFS`, `speed`), 4 Toggle Buttons (`oct`, `Hz`, `harm`, `kb.t`)
    * **EQ:** 1 Dropdown (curve shape), 1 Large Knob (`MIX`)

### **4. Bottom Left Panel: Global Controls & Performance**
This handles the macro states, parts, and physical inputs.

* **Global Group**
    * 2 Part Selector Buttons: `A`, `B`
    * 2 Standard Knobs: `legato limit`, `porta`
    * 1 Toggle Button: `vel`
    * 1 Toggle Button: `link` (chain icon)
* **LFO / Mod Sliders**
    * 1 Top Knob: `vel` (routed to LFO)
    * 3 Vertical Sliders: `vol`, `pitch` (with `pre`/`post fx` labels)
* **X/Y/Z Mod Pad**
    * 1 2D Interactive Grid Component (`MOD`)
    * 3 Standard Knobs below grid: `x`, `y`, `z`
* **Strum Group**
    * 1 Dropdown Menu: Direction (`Up`)
    * 2 Standard Knobs: `time`, `tns`
* **Info Bar**
    * 1 Text Area Panel displaying current parameter info/tooltips.

### **5. Bottom Right Panel: Modulation Editor (Graph)**
This is the large visual editing space for envelopes and LFOs.

* **Header / Routing**
    * 4 Tab Buttons: `ENV`, `IMG`, `FX`, `ADV`
    * 1 Target Dropdown Path: `Phaser offset` -> `LFO`
    * Various small tool buttons for node editing (snap, step, curve).
* **Main Graph Area**
    * Large Cartesian grid component displaying a vector waveform.
* **Graph Modifiers (Bottom Strip)**
    * 4 Standard Knobs: `SPD`, `TNS`, `SKEW`, `PW`
    * 2 Radio/Toggle Buttons: `TEMPO`, `GLOBAL`
    * Viewport tools (bottom right corner): Freeze, Zoom, Grid snap.

**Prompting Tip for Claude:** When you pass this list over to Claude, specify that you want this broken down into `juce::Component` subclasses for each major panel (e.g., `class TimbreSection : public juce::Component`), and ask it to use `juce::FlexBox` or `juce::Grid` inside their respective `resized()` methods to handle the positioning. This will keep your `MainComponent` clean and manageable.
