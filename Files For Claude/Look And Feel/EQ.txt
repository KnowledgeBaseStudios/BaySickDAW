This breakdown focuses on the **Parametric EQ 2** interface from **EQ.jpg**. Since you are using the specific mechanical logic of this EQ for your build, I have detailed the functional specs for every control cluster to ensure the "look and feel" translates into a working JUCE replica.

---

## 1. The Main EQ Grid & Nodes
* **Visual**: A dark, slate-blue background with a logarithmic frequency grid. 
* **Nodes (1–7)**: Each node is a colored ring with a number.
    * **Logic**: Vertical movement = **Gain** (+/- 18dB); Horizontal movement = **Frequency** (20Hz–20kHz).
    * **Interaction**: Mouse-wheel on a node should adjust the **Bandwidth (Q)**, widening or narrowing the filter curve.
    * **Color-Sync**: The color of the node ring must match the corresponding slider in the right-hand panel.

---

## 2. The Right-Hand Control Strip (The "Mixer" Section)
This is a high-density vertical control bank for individual band management.

### **Top Buttons (Band Toggle)**
* **Visual**: Small colored squares matching nodes 1–7.
* **Logic**: **Band On/Off Bypass**. Clicking toggles the active state of that specific EQ filter.

### **The Faders (Gain)**
* **Visual**: Slim vertical tracks with a small square thumb.
* **Logic**: **Precision Gain Control**. This mirrors the vertical position of the node on the grid.

### **The "Center" Circles (Filter Type)**
* **Visual**: A row of small gray circles between the faders and lower dials.
* **Logic**: **Filter Shape Selector**. Clicking/Scrolling here cycles through Low Shelf, Peaking, High Shelf, Low Pass, High Pass, Band Pass, and Notch.

### **The Lower Dials (Frequency & Bandwidth)**
* **Top Row (Freq)**: Adjusts the center frequency of the band.
* **Bottom Row (BW/Q)**: Adjusts the steepness or width of the filter curve.

---

## 3. Bottom Global Controls (Toolbar)
* **Play/Pause Icon**: Toggles the real-time **Spectrum Analyzer** overlay on the grid.
* **LIN (Linear Phase)**: Toggles between standard IIR filters and Linear Phase mode (useful for mastering).
* **HQ (High Quality)**: Enables **Oversampling** to reduce aliasing in the high frequencies.
* **Monitor**: Changes the analyzer view (e.g., Peaks vs. Average).
* **Compare**: An **A/B Switch** to quickly toggle between two different EQ states.
