## 1. Visual Schematic & Geometry

### **The Meter Strip**
* **Structure**: A narrow vertical rectangular column with a dark, recessed background.
* **Tick Marks**: Horizontal lines extending across the width of the meter, corresponding to specific dB levels.
* **Indicator Bar**: A vertical bar (usually segmented or solid) that rises from the bottom. The color typically transitions from dark green to yellow, then red at the "0" mark to indicate clipping.

### **Typography & Alignment**
* **Placement**: Numbers are centered vertically between or slightly above their respective tick marks.
* **Font**: Narrow, condensed sans-serif (e.g., Roboto Condensed or specialized "Digital" fonts) to maximize readability in a slim space.

## 3. Special Interaction & Usage Logic

### **Universal Implementation**
* **Logic Instruction**: This meter design should be treated as the **standard component** for any effect requiring a dB or dBFS readout. 
* **Space Saving (Truncation)**: If the plugin UI is vertically constrained, the meter can be **truncated at -20dBFS**. The range should focus on the "action zone" (0 to -20) to maintain resolution for critical leveling decisions.

### **Ballistics & Decay**
* **Logic**: Implement "Peak Hold" logic. When a signal hits a level, a single-pixel line should remain at that height for ~1000ms before falling slowly, allowing the user to see the absolute peaks of the audio.
* **Refresh Rate**: To ensure a "smooth" analog feel, the meter drawing should be updated at **60fps** (every ~16.6ms) using a `juce::Timer`.
