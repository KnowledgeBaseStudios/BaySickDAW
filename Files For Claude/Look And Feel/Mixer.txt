## 1. Visual Schematic & Geometry

### **The Channel Strip (Per Track)**
* **Track Header**: Small rectangular area at the top containing the track index number (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Peak Meter**: A long, ultra-slim vertical rectangle. It features a dark background with thin, dimmed horizontal tick marks.
* **Panning Knob**: A small, circular "Modern Domed" knob located just above the fader. It features a center-detent indicator (a small green LED glow that appears when centered).
* **The Main Fader**: 
    * **Track**: A vertical line etched into the panel.
    * **Thumb**: A rectangular, "cap-style" slider with a subtle horizontal highlight to indicate the grip.
    * **Level Indicator**: As the fader moves, a thin neon-green line follows the thumb from the bottom to the current position.
* **The Track Footer**: Contains the "Record Arm" (disk icon), "Mute/Solo" (light bulb icon), and track naming label.

### **Routing & Bottom Icons**
* **Routing Wire**: A curved, neon-green line that visually connects the bottom of one channel to another (usually the Master), indicating signal flow.
* **Phase Invert / Swap Channels**: Small triangular icons at the very bottom of the strip used for signal polarity.

## 3. Special Interaction & Usage Logic

### **The "Magnetism" Logic**
* **Fader Snapping**: Implement logic where holding `Alt` allows for fine-grain movement (0.1dB increments), while a standard drag moves in 0.5dB increments.
* **Pan Reset**: A double-click on the panning knob must instantly snap the value back to 0 (Center).

### **Routing Logic**
* **Visual Cable**: The "Routing Wire" is not just a line; it is a `juce::Path` drawn with a Bezier curve (`cubicTo`). 
* **Logic**: The wire should only appear when a track is "armed" to send signal to another track. The thickness of the line could subtly pulse based on the audio amplitude being sent.
