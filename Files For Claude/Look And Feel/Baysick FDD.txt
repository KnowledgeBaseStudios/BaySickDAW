This is the master blueprint for **BaySickSynth**, based on the architecture of the FL Studio MiniSynth. 

Per your strict instructions, **I have completely excised all effects modules (Delay, Chorus, Reverb, etc.)** from this architecture. What remains is the pure synthesis engine: the Oscillator, the Filter, their respective Envelopes, and the LFO.

This interface relies on a sleek, flat, modern vector aesthetic characterized by a central dynamic graph screen and tabbed navigation. 

---

## 1. Visual Schematic & Geometry (The "BaySickSynth" Aesthetic)

### **The Layout Architecture**
* **Header / Top Bar**: Contains the plugin name ("BaySickSynth"), global volume, and a master pitch/pan control if desired.
* **The Central Visualizer (The Screen)**: A large, dark rectangular window occupying the top half of the UI. This is a dynamic graph that changes depending on the active tab (e.g., showing a waveform, or an ADSR envelope curve).
* **The Navigation Bar**: A horizontal row of flat, selectable tabs positioned below the visualizer screen (`OSC`, `OSC ENV`, `FILTER`, `FLT ENV`, `LFO`). 
* **The Control Deck**: The bottom half of the UI. The knobs and dropdowns here change based on which tab is selected.

### **UI Components**
* **The "Flat Ring" Knobs**: Minimalist 2D knobs. 
    * *Background Track*: A thin, dark gray circular line.
    * *Active Arc*: A thicker, brightly colored line that fills the track from the minimum value to the current value.
    * *Label & Value*: Displayed cleanly below or inside the knob (e.g., "Attack: 45ms").
* **Dropdown Menus**: Clean, dark rectangular boxes with an arrow on the right. Clicking opens a flat list of options with a highlighted hover state.
* **The Dynamic Graph**: Uses `juce::Path` to draw bright, anti-aliased vector lines against a near-black background grid.

## 3. Tab-by-Tab Schematics & Parameters

Here is the exact mapping of all the DSP parameters and controls required for each tab.

### **TAB 1: OSCILLATOR (`OSC`)**
* **Visualizer State**: Displays the actual shape of the selected waveform.
* **Controls**:
    * **Waveform Dropdown**: `Sine`, `Triangle`, `Square`, `Saw`, `Pulse`.
    * **Sub-Oscillator Knob**: Adds a sine/square wave one octave below the root.
    * **Pulse Width (PW)**: Only active if `Square/Pulse` is selected; alters the duty cycle.
    * **Unison / Voices**: Dropdown or slider (1 to 8 voices) for detuned thickness.
    * **Detune**: Spreads the pitch of the unison voices.
    * **Glide (Portamento)**: Knob adjusting the slide time between overlapping notes (0ms to ~2000ms).
    * **Pan & Volume**: Base positioning and output level of the oscillator.

### **TAB 2: OSCILLATOR ENVELOPE (`OSC ENV`)**
* **Visualizer State**: Displays an ADSR graph. The points of the graph must move visually as the knobs are turned.
* **Controls** (Amplitude Envelope):
    * **Attack Knob**: Time to reach peak volume (0ms to 10s).
    * **Decay Knob**: Time to drop to sustain level (0ms to 10s).
    * **Sustain Knob**: Holding volume level (0% to 100%).
    * **Release Knob**: Fade out time after note-off (0ms to 10s).

### **TAB 3: FILTER (`FILTER`)**
* **Visualizer State**: Displays a frequency response curve (Bode plot) showing the cutoff slope and resonance peak.
* **Controls**:
    * **Filter Type Dropdown**: `Low Pass`, `High Pass`, `Band Pass`, `Notch`.
    * **Cutoff Knob**: Frequency point of the filter (e.g., 20Hz to 20kHz).
    * **Resonance Knob**: Emphasizes the frequencies at the cutoff point (creates the "peak").
    * **Env Amount Knob (Bipolar)**: Determines how much the Filter Envelope modulates the cutoff (-100% to +100%). 
    * **Keyboard Track (KB)**: How much the cutoff frequency follows the pitch of the MIDI notes played (0% to 100%).

### **TAB 4: FILTER ENVELOPE (`FLT ENV`)**
* **Visualizer State**: Displays an ADSR graph specifically for the filter cutoff movement.
* **Controls** (Filter Modulation Envelope):
    * **Attack Knob**: (0ms to 10s).
    * **Decay Knob**: (0ms to 10s).
    * **Sustain Knob**: (0% to 100%).
    * **Release Knob**: (0ms to 10s).

### **TAB 5: LFO (`LFO`)**
* **Visualizer State**: Displays a continuous scrolling wave matching the LFO shape and speed.
* **Controls**:
    * **Shape Dropdown**: `Sine`, `Triangle`, `Square`, `Saw Down`, `Saw Up`, `Sample & Hold (Random)`.
    * **Destination Dropdown**: Where the LFO is sent (`Pitch`, `Cutoff`, `Volume`, `Pan`).
    * **Rate / Speed Knob**: How fast the LFO oscillates. Needs a toggle for `Hz` (Free) vs. `Sync` (Beat divisions like 1/4, 1/8).
    * **Amount Knob (Bipolar)**: Depth of the modulation (-100% to +100%).
    * **Phase/Delay Knob**: Time delay before the LFO begins oscillating after a key is pressed.

## 4. Special Interaction & Logic Requirements

### **The "Live Graph" Updating**
* **Logic**: This is the defining feature of the look and feel. The central `VisualizerScreen` class must listen to the `AudioProcessorValueTreeState` (APVTS). If the user is on the `OSC ENV` tab and turns the `Attack` knob, the first segment of the `juce::Path` line must physically stretch out to the right in real-time.

### **Bipolar Arcs**
* **Logic**: For parameters like **Filter Env Amount**, **LFO Amount**, and **Pan**, the "Flat Ring" knobs must originate at 12 o'clock. Moving the knob left fills the arc to the left (often in a different color, like red/orange) and moving it right fills it to the right (green/blue).
