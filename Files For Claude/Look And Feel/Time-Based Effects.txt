### **The "Momentum" Drag**
Because these knobs are large and "heavy," the interaction should feel substantial.
* **Logic**: Implement a "friction" variable. The `Slider` should require more mouse movement pixels per degree of rotation than a standard knob, giving the user a sense of precision and weight.

* **Logic**: The UI should never allow these levers to sit "between" values. Use a `juce::AudioParameterChoice` and ensure the `draw` call only renders the lever at specific angles (e.g., -45°, 0°, 45°).
