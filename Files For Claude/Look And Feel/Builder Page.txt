This final breakdown focuses on the **Playlist/Arrangement** view, which we will call the **Builder Page**. Based on **Builder Page Pattern.png**, **Audio Clip.png**, and **Automation Clip.png**, this is the structural heart of the DAW where patterns, audio, and automation are sequenced into a song.

---

## 1. Visual Schematic & Geometry

### **The Track Header (Left Column)**
* **Structure**: Stacked rectangular blocks (Track 1, Track 2, etc.) with a dark matte finish.
* **Active Indicator**: A small circular LED on the right side of each track header that glows neon green (`#A0DB2B`) when the track is active or receiving signal.
* **Naming**: Small, clean sans-serif text aligned to the left.

### **The Source Picker (Far Left Sidebar)**
* **Tabs**: Three distinct icons at the top representing **Patterns** (Piano Roll icon), **Audio Clips** (Waveform icon), and **Automation Clips** (Node/Line icon).
* **List View**: A vertical container where available clips are listed. Selected items feature a thin border or highlight.

### **The Arrangement Grid (Main Area)**
* **Background**: A dark, uniform slate-gray grid.
* **Vertical Timeline**: 
    * **Bar Markers**: Thick vertical lines with numbers (1, 2, 3...) at the top.
    * **Beat Markers**: Subtle, thinner vertical lines dividing bars into smaller increments.
* **Clips on Grid**: Rectangular blocks with a 1px border. 
    * **Pattern Clips**: Show a "miniature" version of the Piano Roll notes inside the block.
    * **Audio Clips**: Display a waveform representation.
    * **Automation Clips**: Display a line with nodes representing the parameter curve.

## 3. Special Interaction & Rendering Logic

### **The "Tab-Switching" Logic**
* **State Management**: The Source Picker must dynamically swap its contents based on the selected tab (Pattern/Audio/Automation). Use a `juce::Viewport` to handle scrolling through long lists of clips.

### **The "Ghost" Placement**
* **Logic**: When dragging a clip from the Source Picker onto the grid, a semi-transparent "ghost" version of the clip should follow the mouse, snapping to the nearest grid line (Bar/Beat) before placement.

### **Miniature Previews**
* **Pattern Drawing**: Inside a pattern clip on the grid, draw simplified MIDI notes as 1px horizontal lines.
* **Waveform Drawing**: For audio clips, use a low-resolution thumbnail of the audio file to draw the waveform envelope.
